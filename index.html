Win Go is the most played game on BDG Play. It is a colour prediction game that runs in short timed rounds — choose from 1 minute, 3 minute, or 5 minute sessions. K3 Lottery is a dice-based number prediction game. Three dice determine the round result, and players make their predictions before the draw closes. The format is straightforward and familiar, making it a popular choice among users who enjoy traditional lottery-style gameplay on the BDG Play platform.

5D Lottery

5D Lottery builds on the standard lottery format by using five numbers per round. This increases the number of possible combinations and gives players more options to choose from. It suits users who enjoy detailed, number-based prediction games with extra depth and variety in each round.

Aviator Game

Aviator is a real-time multiplayer crash game. An aircraft takes off and a multiplier keeps increasing as it climbs. Players must decide when to cash out before the plane flies away and the round ends. Timing is critical — cash out too early and you leave money on the table; wait too long and you lose.

TRX Hash Win is a blockchain-based prediction game where results are determined using real transaction hash data from the TRON network. Because results come from blockchain activity, outcomes are independently verifiable and transparent — a feature that many players appreciate for its openness.
